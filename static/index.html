🔒 What happens to my data?
  • A single CSV is kept in the server's memory while you work. It isn't saved to a database, and it's dropped when the app restarts or newer uploads push it out.
  • Files you put together are saved in a temporary folder on the server, and deleted after 6 hours or when newer uploads push them out, whichever comes first.
  • The statistics are done by fixed, tested code. The AI never does the math.
  • Only if you type your own question, or ask for suggested steps, the AI sees your column names, their types and up to six example values per column, with IDs, emails and phone numbers hidden. It never sees your full file or your results. Picking a prepared question sends nothing to the AI.
  • Avoid uploading sensitive data to a shared demo.
